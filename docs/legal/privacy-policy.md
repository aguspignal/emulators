# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 13, 2026

## 1. Information we collect

**None.** The App does not ask you to create an account, does not request your name, email address, or any other personal details, and does not transmit any information to us. We operate no servers and have no ability to see your games, saves, settings, or anything else about your use of the App.

The App contains no analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising software, and it does not access or use the device's advertising ID, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or any similar data.

## 2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following information locally in its private storage on your device. This information is not transmitted to us or to third parties, except for the cover-art requests described in Section 3:

Game files (ROMs) you import.
Game library information, including file names, display names, game titles, content hashes (MD5 and SHA-1), import and play timestamps, and favorite status.
Save data, including in-game saves and up to ten save-state slots per game. Save states may include a small screenshot used as a menu thumbnail.
Cover-art and icon images associated with your games.
App settings, such as controller vibration preferences.

We cannot access or view this locally stored data. It remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App, as described in Section 7.

Android backups. Depending on your device and account settings, Android may include App data in backups associated with your Google account. These backups are provided and controlled by Android/Google and are subject to Google's own privacy practices.

## 3. Internet Access and Cover Art

The App may connect to the Internet to download cover-art images for games in your library. These requests are made to servers operated by the libretro project, an independent third party.

To request a cover image, the App does not send your game files, file contents, file hashes, or other personal information. However, as with any Internet connection, the server may receive your device's IP address and standard connection information. The name of the requested image may also reveal the title of a game in your library.

We do not receive or have access to information contained in the libretro project's server logs. The libretro project operates independently from us and is not paid, affiliated with, or directed by us.

Cover-art requests may involve the transfer of information to servers located in countries other than your own. For more information about how libretro handles information, please refer to its Privacy Policy.

Cover art is optional. If the App cannot connect to the Internet, it remains fully functional and simply displays games without cover art.

## 4. Third-party services

- **libretro thumbnail server**: serves cover-art images, as described in Section 3. Receives: your IP address, connection metadata, and requested image names. Purpose: displaying box art in your library.
- **Google Play**: distributes and updates the App. Google may collect data in connection with your use of Google Play (such as install statistics and device information) under its own terms and privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not control this and receive from Google at most aggregated, non-identifying statistics about installs and crashes that Google chooses to share with developers.

The App embeds no other third-party SDKs or services that transmit data. It contains no cookies or similar tracking technologies.

## 5. Permissions

- **Internet access**: used solely to download cover art (Section 3).
- **Vibration**: used to provide optional haptic feedback. This can be turned off in the App's settings and involves no data collection.
- **Reading files you pick**: when you import a game, Android's system file picker grants the App one-time access to the specific file you chose. The App does not request broad storage permissions and cannot browse your files.

The App requests no other permissions, and none of Android's sensitive permission groups.

## 6. Data security

- The App's data is stored in Android's private storage, which the operating system isolates from other applications.
- Cover-art downloads use HTTPS (encryption in transit).
- Because we hold no user data, there is no developer-side database, no accounts to secure, and no possibility of a breach of our systems exposing your data.

Local data is protected by your device's own security (screen lock, device encryption). Keeping the device itself secure is in your hands.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

Everything the App stores is on your device and under your direct control. Uninstalling the App, or using Android's "Clear storage" for the App, permanently deletes all App data — games, saves, settings, and images. We retain nothing, because we never had anything.

There are no accounts, so there is no account-deletion procedure and no data-deletion request to make: deletion is something you perform directly, and it takes effect immediately. The only copies that may outlive this are device backups made by Android to your Google account (Section 2), which you manage through your Google account settings.

## 8. Your Privacy Rights

Applicable data-protection laws may give you rights concerning personal data, including the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, as well as other rights that may apply depending on your location.

We do not collect or hold personal data about you ourselves. The App's locally stored data remains on your device and is under your control as described in Section 7. As a result, there is generally no personal data held by us on which we could act in response to a data-access, correction, or deletion request.

If you believe that we hold personal data about you, or if you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact us using the details in Section 12. Where applicable law gives you the right to do so, you may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

Third-party providers, such as Google Play or other services that may process information independently of us, are responsible for their own processing of your personal data. For requests concerning data held by those providers, you should contact the relevant provider directly.

## 9. Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children. Because the App collects no personal data from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children, and there is nothing for a parent or guardian to request deletion of. If you have questions about a child's use of the App, contact the address in Section 12.

## 10. Legal disclosures and business transfers

We hold no user data, so there is nothing we could produce in response to a legal request, and nothing that could be transferred with the App in a merger, acquisition, sale of assets, or similar transaction. If the App's practices ever change such that data is collected, this Policy will be updated first (Section 11), and any subsequently collected data would be disclosed only where required by valid legal process or transferred as part of such a transaction under a policy at least as protective as this one.

## 11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Policy may be revised from time to time — in particular, it **will** be revised before any change that causes the App to collect data it does not collect today. The current version, with its effective date, is always available at the address linked from the App's Google Play listing. Material changes will be indicated by a new effective date and a summary of what changed. The App has no way to notify you individually (it has no accounts or push notifications), so please review this page when the App updates.
